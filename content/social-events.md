## Social Dinner
<!--La cena sarà alle 19:30. Dove inserisco l'orario? -->
The social dinner will take place on the evening of the first day of the workshop (3rd September) at 19:30 at the **Teatro Lirico** of Cagliari ([Via Sant'Alenixedda, 09131 Cagliari CA, Sardinia (Italy)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3b1d7f8Z5g2k4x9h6)).
The Teatro Lirico is Cagliari’s main opera house, known for its vibrant cultural programming and elegant setting. Adjacent to the theatre is the **Parco della Musica**, a modern urban park that enhances the area’s cultural atmosphere and offers a pleasant space for pre- or post-dinner strolls.

## Botanical Garden Visit

The [Botanical Garden](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/explore/botanical-garden-cagliari) ([Via Sant'Ignazio da Laconi, 11, Cagliari, Sardinia (Italy)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WdMy8c2ZJhTi2bLGA)) in Cagliari is a vast green space located in the city's historic center. It hosts thousands of plant species from around the world, including some that are very rare. At its heart lies an archaeological area containing numerous Roman remains. The garden is not only a fascinating natural retreat but also an important part of the University of Cagliari’s urban heritage.
