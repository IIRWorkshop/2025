## Directions

The Sant'Ignazio Campus is located in the city centre and is 16 minutes away by walk and 11 minutes away by bus from the [Cagliari’s train station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XRFRr5dZ5d9A2WkU7). 
The workshop venue can also be easily reached by bus or by taxi from [Piazza Matteotti](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QVygaUhj6LgJ6jaSA), 
which is the central square of Cagliari (in front of the Cagliari Port and the train station). From Piazza Matteotti, you can take the CTM’s local 
bus No. 8 to get Buoncammino (angolo Belvedere) and reach the venue by continuing downhill, or take bus No. 5/5ZE to get to Merello (angolo San Giovanni Bosco) and proceed with a short walk to the venue.
The departing bus stop is on the west side of Piazza Matteotti. For more information, please look at the [Google Maps route](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WLQYLKWSJHWtnbLB9) from the Cagliari’s train 
station to the workshop venue.

For information about reaching Sardinia (and Cagliari), visit the [IIR 2025 travel information](/2025/travel-information/) page.
